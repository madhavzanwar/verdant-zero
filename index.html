How to play

Mission

  • Plant all 28 planters before the 10:00 timer runs out. Each seed costs 25 water.
  • Collect glowing water drops (+25) on sidewalks, alleys and skyways.
  • Planters hide on the street, in alleys, on skyways, on garden rooftops and out in the smog wastes. Jump, then hold Space to hover up to ledges.
  • Chain plantings within 30s for a ×2 → ×4 → ×8 combo. Planting in smog or acid rain doubles points.

Threats

  • Smog drains health, slows you and hurts young vines.
  • Acid rain rolls in every couple of minutes — shelter under skyways or awnings.
  • Purge drones burn vines and shoot you once they spot you (14m). Watch for the red aim line, then dash aside.
  • The Light Pulse clears smog (15m) and stuns drones for 6s. 10s cooldown.
